Viktoria Noel

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Tübingen, working on nonequilibrium quantum many-body physics. My research explores the emergence of universal and collective behaviour from microscopic interactions in strongly correlated systems, as well as ways to harness dissipation in open quantum systems. My work combines analytical and numerical methods from quantum field theory and phase-space approaches, including joint projects with experimental groups.
